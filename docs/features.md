# Feature Engineering Reference: Bengaluru Rainwater Harvesting

This document details the engineered feature sets, their mathematical definitions, hydrological rationale, and temporal leakage prevention design.

---

## 1. Hierarchy of Temporal Scales

To estimate both daily storage dynamics and seasonal/monthly harvestable yields, features are constructed across three synchronized temporal tiers:

| Tier | Granularity | Key Prediction Targets | Hydrological Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Daily** | $1\text{ day}$ ($N=45,656$) | Daily depth $P_t$, occurrence $W_t$, dry spell duration $L_{\text{dry}}$ | First-flush rejection, gutter sizing, tank spillover, and continuous daily water balance simulation. |
| **Tier 2: Monthly** | $1\text{ month}$ ($N=1,500$) | Monthly yield $P_{\text{month}}$, wet day count, peak storm depth | Monthly water demand offset percentage and dry-period supply deficits. |
| **Tier 3: Seasonal** | 4 meteorological seasons ($N=500$) | Seasonal yield $P_{\text{season}}$ ($q_{10}$ dry, $q_{50}$ normal, $q_{90}$ wet) | Sizing seasonal recharge pits and planning bulk storage capacity. |

## 2. Daily Features (`features_bengaluru_daily.csv`)

Generated by `add_calendar_features()`, `add_antecedent_memory_features()`, and `add_spatial_gradient_features()`.

### A. Harmonic Calendar & Phenology
1. **Day-of-Year Harmonic Encodings**:
   $$\sin_{\text{doy}}(t) = \sin\left(\frac{2\pi \cdot DOY_t}{365.25}\right), \quad \cos_{\text{doy}}(t) = \cos\left(\frac{2\pi \cdot DOY_t}{365.25}\right)$$
   - *Rationale*: Eliminates the artificial discontinuity between Dec 31 ($DOY=365$) and Jan 1 ($DOY=1$).
2. **Season Progression Index**:
   $$\text{Prog}_{\text{season}}(t) = \frac{t - t_{\text{season\_start}}}{t_{\text{season\_end}} - t_{\text{season\_start}}} \in [0, 1]$$
   - *Rationale*: Captures monsoon onset, peak, and withdrawal stages.

### B. Antecedent Memory & Persistence ($\le t-1$)
3. **Antecedent Dry Weather Period ($ADWP$)**:
   $$ADWP_t = \begin{cases} 0, & \text{if } P_{t-1} \ge \tau \\ ADWP_{t-1} + 1, & \text{if } P_{t-1} < \tau \end{cases} \quad (\tau = 0.1\text{ mm})$$
   - *Rationale*: Measures consecutive antecedent dry days. Longer dry spells cause dust accumulation, directly governing first-flush diversion depth ($FF$) and reducing initial runoff efficiency.
4. **Multi-Horizon Rolling Precipitation**:
   $$P_{\text{roll}, k}(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{k} P_{t-i}, \quad k \in \{3, 7, 14, 30\}\text{ days}$$
   - *Rationale*: Quantifies atmospheric moisture buildup and intra-seasonal monsoon oscillations.
5. **Rolling Volatility ($CV_{14}$)**:
   $$CV_{14}(t) = \frac{\sigma_{14}(P_{t-1}, \dots, P_{t-14})}{\mu_{14}(P_{t-1}, \dots, P_{t-14}) + \epsilon}$$
   - *Rationale*: Distinguishes continuous gentle monsoon rainfall from volatile convective thunderstorms.
6. **2-Day Markov State**:
   $$\text{State}_{\text{Markov}}(t) = 2 \cdot \mathbb{I}(P_{t-2} \ge \tau) + \mathbb{I}(P_{t-1} \ge \tau) \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$$
   - *Rationale*: Supplies the prior state for a two-stage (occurrence + depth) precipitation generator.

### C. Spatial Gradients ($\le t-1$)
7. **East-West and North-South Asymmetry**:
   $$\Delta_{\text{E-W}}(t-1) = P_{\text{east}, t-1} - P_{\text{central}, t-1}$$
   $$\Delta_{\text{N-S}}(t-1) = P_{\text{central}, t-1} - P_{\text{south}, t-1}$$
8. **Spatial Coherence Ratio**:
   $$\rho_{\text{spatial}}(t-1) = \frac{\min(P_{\text{central}}, P_{\text{south}}, P_{\text{east}})_{t-1}}{\max(P_{\text{central}}, P_{\text{south}}, P_{\text{east}})_{t-1} + \epsilon}$$
   - *Rationale*: Differentiates regional depressions ($\rho \to 1$) from hyper-localized convective cells ($\rho \to 0$).

## 3. Monthly Features (`features_bengaluru_monthly.csv`)

Generated by `build_monthly_features()`.

- **Targets**:
  - `monthly_rain_mm`: Total monthly rainfall depth.
  - `wet_days_count`: Number of rainy days ($\ge 0.1\text{ mm}$).
  - `max_daily_storm_mm`: Peak 24-hour storm in that month.
- **Predictors (Strictly Lagged to Prior Months)**:
  - `month_sin`, `month_cos`: Harmonic annual coordinates ($\sin(2\pi m / 12), \cos(2\pi m / 12)$).
  - `historical_normal_rain_mm`: Long-term monthly climate normal.
  - `rain_lag1m`, `rain_lag2m`: Total rainfall observed in month $m-1$ and $m-2$.
  - `wet_days_lag1m`: Count of rain days in month $m-1$.
  - `rolling_3m_sum`: Cumulative precipitation over the prior 3 months.
  - `rolling_12m_sum`: Cumulative precipitation over the prior 12 months (annual deficit / drought baseline).
  - `departure_pct_lag1m`: Prior month departure percentage relative to its historical normal.

## 4. Seasonal Features (`features_bengaluru_seasonal.csv`)

Generated by `build_seasonal_features()` across Bengaluru's 4 meteorological seasons:
1. **Winter**: January – February (lean dry season).
2. **Pre-Monsoon**: March – May (convective thunderstorm onset).
3. **SW-Monsoon**: June – September (primary monsoon recharge).
4. **NE-Post-Monsoon**: October – December (retreating monsoon, intense autumn storms).

- **Targets**:
  - `seasonal_rain_mm`: Cumulative seasonal yield.
  - `wet_days_count`, `max_daily_storm_mm`.
- **Predictors**:
  - `season_sin`, `season_cos`: Harmonic coordinates ($\sin(2\pi s / 4), \cos(2\pi s / 4)$).
  - `rain_lag1s`: Total rainfall of the immediately preceding season.
  - `rolling_4s_annual_sum`: Cumulative rolling 4-season (12-month) precipitation.
  - `pre_monsoon_rain_mm`: Pre-monsoon rainfall amount, **strictly masked to be visible only during SW-Monsoon and NE-Post-Monsoon** (set to `NaN` during Winter and Pre-Monsoon to prevent leakage).

## 5. Temporal Leakage Prevention & Verification

All feature transformers are verified by automated tests in `tests/test_features.py`:
- **Shift Guarantee**: All temporal aggregation functions enforce `shift(1)` so that no observation from step $t$ enters predictors for step $t$.
- **Perturbation Testing**: In unit tests, injecting a 100 mm synthetic storm into day/month $t$ asserts that predictor features at step $t$ remain exactly unchanged ($\Delta = 0$).
